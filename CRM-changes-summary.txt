3. SHIFT VERIFICATION (new)
======================================================================
Only finished shifts can be verified: completed, or a missed clock-in or
clock-out. Future and cancelled shifts get no mark and no buttons.

Marks: one small circle per shift, the same in every view.
  green check  = verified (by the parent or by an admin)
  red X        = not verified yet
  slate clock  = sent to the parent, waiting
  amber ?      = the parent messaged the office about it
- Grid cells: one mark per shift, in the top-right corner of its own color
  band. The hours sit just under the marks (same cell size as before).
- WellSky boxes and Calendar cards: the mark is in the top-right corner.

Buttons, inside each shift's outline (Grid/WellSky hover cards and the
Calendar day panel):
- Verify with parent:
  - Sends the shift to the family's portal.
  - The parent email comes from the client's "Email ID" on the Clients
    tab, matched by UCI, then by name.
- Verify as admin:
  - Asks for the staff member's own PIN.
  - Marks the shift verified in the CRM and records it on the portal's
    office page as "Verified · by admin · name". The family never sees it.
- Unverify (on a verified shift): asks for the PIN, then:
  - If the PARENT verified it: the portal reopens it, and it goes back to
    the family as a normal visit to verify, with the usual notification.
  - If an ADMIN verified it: it goes back to not verified, and the
    portal's office page shows "Unverified · by admin · name".
- Before sending, Verify with parent gets the portal's copy ready (reopen /
  unverify / withdraw as needed). It only says "Sent" once the portal
  really has the visit waiting for the family.

Verification PIN:
- Each staff member sets their own PIN under Your account > Verification
  PIN, with a CRM password check.
- 4 to 6 digits, stored as a scrypt hash.
- 5 wrong tries lock verifying for 10 minutes.

Speed:
- Verify / unverify update the screen immediately.
- Portal updates for admin actions run right after the response.
- The CRM reads parents' answers from the portal every minute while the
  calendar is open, when you return to the tab, and every 5 minutes with
  the cron.

Database (migration 0021, applied):
- cal_verifications: one row per shift (row_key). Holds status, via
  parent/admin, who/when, and the parent's message.
- admin_pins: PIN hashes, server-only.

Vercel environment variable (set):
- CRM_VERIFY_SECRET = the portal's Vault secret crm_verify_secret.
- PORTAL_VERIFY_URL is optional; it defaults to the portal's
  verifications-intake.

4. CRM <-> PORTAL CONNECTION (for the Portal chat)
======================================================================
The CRM uses the portal's verifications-intake, as documented in the
portal's docs/crm-hours-verification.md (migrations 075 and 079):
- POST visits:
  external_id = the CRM's cal_scan_visits.row_key
  plus parent_email, client_name ("First Last"), caregiver_name
  ("First L."), visit_date, clock_in/out, scheduled_start/end (Pacific
  ISO times).
- POST verified_by_admin + verified_at: admin verified (sent with the
  visit details).
- POST unverified_by_admin: admin unverified.
- POST reopen: true: reopen a visit the family verified.
- POST cancelled: true: used only to move an "unverified" visit back to
  the family when Verify with parent is clicked again.
- GET ?since= (polling) and GET ?ids= (checking before and after sending).
Portal pull-to-refresh on Verify hours is live (built by the Portal chat).
