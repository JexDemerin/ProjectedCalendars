This replaces the earlier note. The spec is Jex's message of Oct 21 plus the approved mockup:
https://claude.ai/artifact/UjLgVvxD98Xr5vkYfN48Wb (CRM > Client record, Admin > Family profile, Admin > Notifications)

Parts:
1) Client record form: Mother/Father fields saved as "Mom: ..."/"Dad: ..." lines in Phone (D) and Email ID (E).
   Primary + Secondary caregivers saved to a new "Client Caregivers" tab. Schedule day boxes plus "Specific" in
   Schedule (M). Posting schedule (N) gets a "Copy from schedule" button.
2) Current Status sync, both ways, by UCI. Fixed label list; newest change wins; portal changes are logged as "Portal".
   The CRM gets a new endpoint (x-crm-secret) for the portal to add/remove labels, and pushes its own changes
   to the portal's crm-client-status.
3) Client Caregivers -> portal crm-client-caregivers (on save and on /api/sync). Send the UCI, child name and both
   parent emails.
4) WellSky visits -> portal crm-work-record (caregiver email, UCI, date, start/end, WellSky status). The portal does
   the counting. List names that don't map to a caregiver. Skip isDummy.
5) Enroll to portal -> crm-enroll-caregiver (first name, last initial, email, phone digits). The temp password lasts
   7 days; pressing again issues a fresh one.
6) Keys: caregiver = email, child = UCI.
7) Spreadsheet: never "Make a copy". The Apps Script stays as is.
